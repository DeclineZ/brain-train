Inventory

What we track today

Tracking today lives in our own Supabase database: 15 tables, the Supabase auth log and 5 database functions. Keeping it in-house keeps player and patient data under our control.

Inside the games

What each game records when a level ends

Every finished level saves one row to game_sessions with a raw_data payload. The payload is different for each of the 21 games. Most store totals for the whole level; three store a timeline of individual actions.
